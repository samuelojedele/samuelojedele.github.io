About Me

I build technology to make complicated work simpler.

I'm a systems-minded software engineer with 3 years of experience building backend systems, APIs, database-driven applications and digital products.

What interests me most isn't simply writing code. It's understanding how a process actually works, finding unnecessary complexity and figuring out whether software, automation or AI can remove it.

My experience spans fintech, software engineering, AI/ML, technical education and startup environments. I've built production systems, integrated third-party services, optimized databases, solved technical problems and trained more than 30 software engineering students.

I'm particularly interested in the intersection of AI + automation + better ways of working. I like experimenting, testing ideas quickly and turning useful experiments into things people can actually use.

My default way of thinking

  • Understand the workflow.
  • Find the repetitive parts.
  • Question unnecessary complexity.
  • Decide whether process or technology is the answer.
  • Build the smallest useful solution.
  • Test it, learn and improve it.
